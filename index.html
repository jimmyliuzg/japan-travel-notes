The shape of it

A little city.
A little countryside.

Make room for the energy of a big city, the warmth of southern Japan, and one quiet stay with a bath and a long dinner. Leave the in-between moments open.

No fixed schedule here—just a few themes to help the days find their own rhythm.

A loose itinerary

A few chapters,
not a minute-by-minute plan.

Keep the order flexible. These are anchors to plan around, not fixed bookings.

  1. ARRIVE / SETTLE

    Take the first evening gently

    Get oriented, find an easy meal nearby, and leave the ambitious plans for tomorrow.

  2. CITY / EXPLORE

    Choose a neighborhood each day

    Mix a park or museum with design browsing, coffee, a long lunch, and one special dinner.

  3. REGIONAL / TASTE

    Make room for local food

    Try a noodle shop, seafood, yakitori, and a relaxed food walk. Add a bath or spa if the day feels right.

  4. CRAFT / WANDER

    Spend a day with ceramics

    Browse kilns, studios, and small galleries; keep the schedule light enough for an unplanned stop.

  5. COUNTRYSIDE / REST

    Stay in, soak, and linger

    One quiet inn, a private bath if available, a seasonal dinner, and a slow breakfast.

  6. RETURN / RESET

    Keep the last stretch simple

    Choose a convenient base, pack without rushing, and save energy for the journey home.

The day-by-day

A little structure.
Plenty of breathing room.

The dates and broad route are shown for planning. Flight identifiers, departure city, lodging names, and reservation details are intentionally left out.

Wed · Nov 18

Travel day

Head to Japan

Keep the travel day simple; rest and settle in after arrival.

Thu · Nov 19

Tokyo · arrival

Ease into the city

Arrive, get to the hotel, take a neighborhood walk, and have a casual dinner before an early night.

Fri · Nov 20

Tokyo · vintage & wine

Browse, stroll, then linger over dinner

Start with a run near the Imperial Palace, browse vintage and design shops around Aoyama, Daikanyama, or Ginza, then find a Japanese wine bar and a counter-seat dinner.

Sat · Nov 21

Tokyo · culture

Pick one cultural anchor

Choose a museum or architecture outing, then take an easy Ginza afternoon for a food hall, watches, stationery, or a long lunch.

Sun · Nov 22

Tokyo · flex day

Leave the day open

Wander a coffee-and-design neighborhood, join a food or wine walk, revisit a favorite area, or simply take it slow.

Mon · Nov 23

Tokyo → Fukuoka

Travel south, keep the evening easy

Allow extra time for the airport transfer. After checking in, take a gentle park or art-museum stroll if there is energy, then enjoy a relaxed dinner.

Tue · Nov 24

Fukuoka · food & bath

Eat well, choose one spa

Start with a park run, try local ramen, and take a small-group food walk for seafood, gyoza, and yakitori. Pick either a hotel spa or a public bath, then finish with hot pot.

Wed · Nov 25

Ceramics · onsen country

Make a day of pottery and craft

Visit a ceramics district, kiln, or studio with time for a local lunch, then continue to a countryside inn. Keep the evening for a private bath, seasonal dinner, and rest.

Thu · Nov 26

Onsen country → Fukuoka

Slow morning, simple final night

Enjoy breakfast and a final soak, return to the city, check into a convenient base, pack, and keep dinner low-key.

Fri · Nov 27

Return journey

Leave a generous connection buffer

Travel back to the departure airport with time to collect luggage, check in separately, and handle any delays. Keep the exact flight and airport details off this public page.
